Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026 • Site: careypmediagroup.com

CP Media Group ("we", "us") provides branding, graphics, community-setup and copywriting services for creators, and publishes a free daily briefing, The Daily Creator Pulse. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact and project details: when you email us for a quote, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to share about your project (names, handles, colors, platforms).
  • Newsletter subscription: if you subscribe to The Daily Creator Pulse, we collect your email address and an optional first name or handle.
  • Payments: orders placed through Fiverr are processed by Fiverr. We never receive or store your payment card details.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry, quote and deliver your project.
  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to.
  • We do not sell your information, rent it, or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Newsletter delivery

Newsletter subscriptions are handled by our email delivery provider (Beehiiv) once signups are enabled. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

4. Cookies and third parties

This site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to Fiverr and X, which have their own privacy policies.

5. Retention and your choices

  • We keep project emails only as long as needed to complete and support the work.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing careypmediagroup@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.
  • You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email.

6. Age

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. The effective date above shows the latest revision.
